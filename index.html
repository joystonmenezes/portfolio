About

Health Communication, Misinformation & Measurement

I study how people encounter health information on algorithmic platforms, and how we can measure that exposure reliably. Trained in computer science at USC, I now work in UCLA's Department of Communication (OASIS Lab), where I build and evaluate LLM pipelines for health misinformation. I also collaborate with Prof. Magdalena Wojcieszak's team at UC Davis and the University of Warsaw on news exposure, and at USC ISI I built the data pipelines behind a public dataset of the 2024 U.S. election on TikTok.

Across these projects I keep returning to one question: what do our classifiers, topic models, and LLM judges actually measure, and who do they miss?
